Executive Summary

Volume: Normalized After RIF and Intern Peaks

August solved 2,222 POps tickets, essentially on par with the 2026 average of 2,236. Volume has normalized since the May/June peaks (2,460 and 2,311), which were driven by a RIF-related surge and heavy intern onboarding/offboarding. The April high-water mark (2,507) and Q2 activity created a temporary spike; August represents a return to a sustainable baseline.

  • RIF aftermath: May and June saw elevated ticket volume as the team processed terminations, offboardings, and organizational changes. That wave has largely passed.
  • Intern season: June–July had heavy intern onboarding (and subsequent August offboarding). August still saw intern-related tickets but at a lower rate as the cycle wound down.
  • System efficiencies: Workday self-service adoption and automated greenhouse integrations continue to deflect manually-routed tickets in categories like location updates and comp corrections.

SLA: Best Month Despite Sustained Volume

SLA reached 58.95% — the highest of 2026. This is notable because it improved without a volume drop; the team got faster while handling a normal caseload. EMEA led all regions at 66.9%, while APJC remained the weakest at 44.4%, still far below the 80% target.

CSAT: Scale Change Drove Expected Dip

CSAT fell to 85.5%, the lowest recorded month. However, this was anticipated: the team switched from a binary (2-point) scale in January–March to a 5-point scale in August. A 5-point scale captures more granular sentiment, and "good" ratings naturally become harder to earn when neutral and dissatisfied options are available. Rating volume also dropped — only 76 ratings in Aug vs. 200+ in Jan–Mar — so each neutral or bad score carries more statistical weight.
